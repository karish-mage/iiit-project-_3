Question 41: What does "out-of-pocket maximum" mean in health insurance?
Expected Document: health_policy.txt
Expected Answer: The annual limit of money you have to spend on covered services before insurance pays 100%
Difficulty: Easy
Category: Definitions

Question 42: What is coinsurance?
Expected Document: health_policy.txt (mentions 80% coinsurance, 60% coinsurance)
Expected Answer: Your share of costs for healthcare services after meeting deductible (e.g., you pay 20%, insurance pays 80%)
Difficulty: Easy
Category: Definitions

Question 43: What does "sub-limit" mean?
Expected Document: home_policy.txt
Expected Answer: Lower coverage limit for specific items (e.g., jewelry limited to $2,500 even though total personal property limit is higher)
Difficulty: Medium
Category: Definitions

Question 44: What is a "rider" or "endorsement"?
Expected Document: auto_policy.txt, home_policy.txt
Expected Answer: An addition to a standard insurance policy that modifies or extends coverage (e.g., rideshare endorsement, earthquake rider)
Difficulty: Medium
Category: Definitions

Question 45: What is "prior authorization"?
Expected Document: health_policy.txt
Expected Answer: Insurance company approval required before a service or medication is covered
Difficulty: Easy
Category: Definitions
