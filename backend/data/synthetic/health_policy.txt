SECTION 1: ELIGIBILITY AND ENROLLMENT

1.1 Eligibility Requirements
To be eligible for coverage under this policy, an individual must be a legal resident of the policy's service area, not be entitled to Medicare Part A or enrolled in Medicare Part B, and have submitted a completed enrollment application with all required documentation.

1.2 Enrollment Periods
Open enrollment occurs annually from November 1 through December 15. Special enrollment periods are available for qualifying life events including marriage, birth or adoption of a child, loss of other coverage, and change of residence to the policy's service area.

1.3 Effective Date of Coverage
Coverage becomes effective on the first day of the month following acceptance of the enrollment application, unless the application is received between the 1st and 15th of the month, in which case coverage begins on the first day of the following month.

SECTION 2: COVERED SERVICES

2.1 Preventive Care Services
The following preventive services are covered at 100% with no cost-sharing when provided by in-network providers:
a) Annual physical examinations
b) Immunizations as recommended by the Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices
c) Screenings for cancer (mammography, colonoscopy, Pap smear, prostate screening)
d) Well-child visits and developmental screenings
e) Chronic disease screenings (diabetes, cholesterol, hypertension)
f) Mental health screenings and substance abuse assessments
g) Prenatal care visits

2.2 Hospitalization
Inpatient hospital services are covered subject to the following:
- Semi-private room and board
- Operating room, recovery room, and intensive care unit
- Prescription drugs administered during the stay
- Laboratory tests, diagnostic imaging, and pathology services
- Physical therapy, occupational therapy, and respiratory therapy during the stay

Prior authorization is required for all non-emergency hospital admissions. Failure to obtain prior authorization may result in a 20% reduction in benefits.

2.3 Emergency Services
Emergency services are covered at in-network cost-sharing levels regardless of whether the provider is in-network or out-of-network. An emergency medical condition is defined as a condition manifesting itself by acute symptoms of sufficient severity such that a prudent layperson with average knowledge of health and medicine could reasonably expect the absence of immediate medical attention to result in serious jeopardy to health, serious impairment of bodily functions, or serious dysfunction of any bodily organ or part.

2.4 Prescription Drug Coverage
Prescription drugs are covered according to the following tier structure:
- Tier 1 (Generic): $10 copay
- Tier 2 (Preferred Brand): $35 copay
- Tier 3 (Non-Preferred Brand): $60 copay
- Tier 4 (Specialty): 20% coinsurance up to $250 per prescription

Mail-order prescriptions (90-day supply) are available at 2.5 times the applicable copay. Prior authorization may be required for certain medications as indicated in the formulary.

2.5 Mental Health and Substance Abuse Services
Mental health and substance abuse services are covered at parity with medical/surgical benefits:
a) Outpatient psychotherapy: $25 copay per visit (in-network)
b) Inpatient psychiatric hospitalization: same as medical hospitalization
c) Intensive outpatient programs: $50 copay per session
d) Medication-assisted treatment for substance use disorders
e) Crisis intervention services

SECTION 3: COST-SHARING

3.1 Deductible
Individual Deductible: $3,000 per calendar year
Family Deductible: $6,000 per calendar year

The deductible applies to all covered services except preventive care and prescription drug copays. Once the individual deductible is met, the plan pays its share of covered services for that individual. The family deductible is satisfied when any combination of family members' expenses meets the family deductible amount.

3.2 Coinsurance
After the deductible is met:
- In-Network: Plan pays 80%, member pays 20%
- Out-of-Network: Plan pays 60%, member pays 40%

3.3 Out-of-Pocket Maximum
Individual Out-of-Pocket Maximum: $8,700 per calendar year
Family Out-of-Pocket Maximum: $17,400 per calendar year

Once the out-of-pocket maximum is reached, the plan pays 100% of covered services for the remainder of the calendar year. The out-of-pocket maximum includes deductibles, copays, and coinsurance but does not include premiums, balance-billed charges, or charges for non-covered services.

SECTION 4: EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS

4.1 General Exclusions
This policy does not cover:
a) Cosmetic surgery or procedures unless required for reconstructive purposes following an accident or mastectomy
b) Experimental or investigational treatments not approved by the FDA
c) Services for which the member has no legal obligation to pay
d) Services received before the effective date or after the termination date of coverage
e) Work-related injuries or illnesses covered by workers compensation
f) Dental services except as specifically listed under covered services
g) Vision care except for medical conditions of the eye
h) Long-term custodial care
i) Infertility treatments beyond three cycles of IUI per lifetime
j) Weight loss surgery unless BMI exceeds 40 or BMI exceeds 35 with comorbid conditions

4.2 Pre-Existing Condition Provisions
In accordance with the Affordable Care Act, this policy does not impose any pre-existing condition exclusion period. Coverage for all conditions begins on the effective date of the policy regardless of health status at the time of enrollment.

SECTION 5: CLAIMS AND APPEALS

5.1 Filing Claims
In-network providers will file claims on behalf of the member. For out-of-network services, the member must submit a completed claim form along with an itemized bill within 180 days of the date of service. Claims submitted after 180 days may be denied.

5.2 Appeals Process
If a claim is denied, the member has the right to appeal. The appeals process consists of:
a) Level 1 Internal Appeal: Submit written appeal within 180 days of denial. Decision within 30 days.
b) Level 2 Internal Appeal: If Level 1 is upheld, submit second appeal within 60 days. Decision within 45 days.
c) External Review: If internal appeals are exhausted, request independent external review within 120 days.

5.3 Urgent Care Appeals
For urgent care situations, the plan will expedite the appeals process and provide a decision within 72 hours of receiving the appeal.

SECTION 6: GENERAL PROVISIONS

6.1 Coordination of Benefits
If the member is covered by more than one health plan, this policy will coordinate benefits according to standard coordination of benefits rules. The primary plan pays first; this plan pays the difference up to the amount it would have paid if it were the only plan.

6.2 Subrogation
If the plan pays benefits for injuries caused by a third party, the plan has the right to recover those payments from the third party or from any settlement or judgment the member receives. The member agrees to cooperate with the plan's subrogation efforts and to reimburse the plan from any recovery.

6.3 Termination of Coverage
Coverage terminates on the earliest of:
a) The date the policy is cancelled
b) The date the member ceases to meet eligibility requirements
c) The end of the period for which premium has been paid, if premium is not paid within the 31-day grace period
d) The date the member becomes entitled to Medicare
