Named Insured: Robert and Linda Chen

Construction: Wood Frame with Brick Veneer

SECTION 1: DWELLING COVERAGE (COVERAGE A)

1.1 Covered Perils
We insure against direct physical loss to the dwelling on the residence premises caused by the following perils:
a) Fire or lightning
b) Windstorm or hail (subject to separate wind/hail deductible)
c) Explosion
d) Riot or civil commotion
e) Aircraft or vehicles
f) Smoke
g) Vandalism or malicious mischief
h) Theft
i) Falling objects
j) Weight of ice, snow, or sleet
k) Accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam from household systems
l) Sudden and accidental tearing apart, cracking, burning, or bulging of heating, air conditioning, or fire protection systems
m) Freezing of household systems
n) Sudden and accidental damage from artificially generated electrical current

1.2 Dwelling Coverage Limit
Coverage A Limit: $450,000 (replacement cost basis)

This limit represents the estimated cost to rebuild the dwelling at current construction costs. We recommend reviewing this limit annually to account for construction cost increases. Guaranteed Replacement Cost endorsement is attached, providing up to 125% of the Coverage A limit.

1.3 Building Code Upgrade Coverage
If a covered loss requires repairs that must comply with current building codes, we will pay the additional cost to comply with current building codes, up to an additional 10% of the Coverage A limit.

SECTION 2: OTHER STRUCTURES COVERAGE (COVERAGE B)

2.1 Description
We cover other structures on the residence premises that are separated from the dwelling by clear space, including detached garages, fences, sheds, and swimming pools.

2.2 Coverage Limit
Coverage B Limit: 10% of Coverage A = $45,000

We do not cover other structures used in whole or in part for business purposes, or structures rented to any person not a tenant of the dwelling unless used solely as a private garage.

SECTION 3: PERSONAL PROPERTY COVERAGE (COVERAGE C)

3.1 Covered Property
We cover personal property owned or used by an insured while it is anywhere in the world. Coverage for personal property usually located at another residence is limited to 10% of the Coverage C limit or $1,000, whichever is greater.

3.2 Coverage Limit
Coverage C Limit: 50% of Coverage A = $225,000

3.3 Special Limits of Liability
The following special limits apply:
a) $200 on money, bank notes, coins, and precious metals
b) $1,500 on securities, accounts, deeds, manuscripts, and similar documents
c) $1,500 on watercraft including trailers, furnishings, equipment, and motors
d) $1,500 on trailers not used with watercraft
e) $2,500 on firearms and related equipment
f) $2,500 on silverware, goldware, and pewterware
g) $5,000 on business property on the residence premises
h) $2,500 on business property away from the residence premises
i) $5,000 on jewelry, watches, furs, and precious stones (aggregate limit)
j) $2,500 on electronic data processing equipment and media

SECTION 4: LOSS OF USE COVERAGE (COVERAGE D)

4.1 Additional Living Expenses
If a covered loss makes the residence premises uninhabitable, we will pay the necessary increase in living expenses incurred by the insured to maintain the normal standard of living of the household. Payment will be for the shortest time required to repair or replace the premises, or if the insured permanently relocates, the shortest time for the household to settle elsewhere. Coverage is limited to 24 months.

4.2 Fair Rental Value
If a covered loss makes that part of the residence premises rented to others or held for rental uninhabitable, we will pay the fair rental value of such premises less any expenses that do not continue. Payment is limited to the shortest time required to repair or replace the rented portion.

4.3 Coverage Limit
Coverage D Limit: 20% of Coverage A = $90,000

SECTION 5: LIABILITY COVERAGE (COVERAGE E)

5.1 Personal Liability
If a claim is made or a suit is brought against an insured for damages because of bodily injury or property damage caused by an occurrence to which this coverage applies, we will pay up to our limit of liability for the damages for which the insured is legally liable and defend the insured at our expense.

5.2 Liability Limit
Coverage E Limit: $300,000 per occurrence

5.3 Liability Exclusions
Coverage E does not apply to:
a) Bodily injury or property damage expected or intended by the insured
b) Bodily injury or property damage arising out of business activities of the insured
c) Bodily injury or property damage arising out of the ownership, maintenance, use, or entrustment of aircraft, motor vehicles, or watercraft exceeding specified limits
d) Bodily injury or property damage caused by war, nuclear hazard, or terrorism
e) Liability assumed under any unwritten contract or agreement
f) Property damage to property owned by the insured
g) Bodily injury to any insured under this policy

SECTION 6: MEDICAL PAYMENTS TO OTHERS (COVERAGE F)

6.1 Description
We will pay necessary medical expenses incurred within three years of an accident causing bodily injury to persons other than insureds, when such accident arises out of the insured's premises or activities.

6.2 Coverage Limit
Coverage F Limit: $5,000 per person per accident

SECTION 7: DEDUCTIBLES

7.1 Standard Deductible
All Perils Deductible: $1,000

7.2 Wind/Hail Deductible
Wind and Hail Deductible: 2% of Coverage A = $9,000

The wind/hail deductible applies separately to each wind or hail loss and is calculated based on the Coverage A limit at the time of loss.

7.3 Water Backup Deductible
Water backup and sump pump overflow losses are subject to a $2,500 deductible.

SECTION 8: EXCLUSIONS

8.1 General Exclusions
We do not insure for loss caused directly or indirectly by:
a) Flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of water, or spray from any of these, whether or not driven by wind
b) Earth movement, including earthquake, volcanic eruption, landslide, mudflow, subsidence, and sinkhole
c) Power failure originating outside the residence premises
d) Neglect of the insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property during and after a loss
e) War, including undeclared war, civil war, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, or warlike act
f) Nuclear hazard
g) Intentional loss committed by or at the direction of an insured
h) Government action, including seizure, confiscation, and destruction of property
i) Mold, fungus, or wet rot unless resulting from a covered peril
j) Ordinance or law, except as provided under Building Code Upgrade Coverage

SECTION 9: CONDITIONS

9.1 Duties After Loss
In case of a loss to covered property, the insured must:
a) Give prompt notice to us or our agent
b) Protect the property from further damage and keep an accurate record of repair expenses
c) Cooperate with us in the investigation of a claim
d) Prepare an inventory of damaged personal property showing quantity, description, actual cash value, and amount of loss
e) Submit to examination under oath as often as we reasonably require
f) Produce records and documents as requested and permit copies to be made
g) File a signed, sworn proof of loss within 60 days after our request

9.2 Loss Settlement
Covered losses to the dwelling and other structures will be settled at replacement cost without deduction for depreciation, provided the insured repairs or replaces the damaged property within 180 days. If the insured does not repair or replace within 180 days, settlement will be on an actual cash value basis.

9.3 Mortgage Clause
If a mortgage is shown in the Declarations, loss payable under Coverage A or B will be paid to the mortgagee as interests appear. The mortgagee's right of recovery will not be impaired by any act or neglect of the insured.
