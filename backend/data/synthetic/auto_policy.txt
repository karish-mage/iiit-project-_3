SECTION 1: LIABILITY COVERAGE

1.1 Bodily Injury Liability
We will pay damages for bodily injury or death for which any insured becomes legally responsible because of an auto accident. We will settle or defend, as we consider appropriate, any claim or suit asking for these damages. Our duty to settle or defend ends when our limit of liability for this coverage has been exhausted by payment of judgments or settlements.

Limits of Liability:
- Per Person: $100,000
- Per Accident: $300,000

1.2 Property Damage Liability
We will pay damages for which any insured becomes legally responsible because of damage to or destruction of property, including loss of its use, arising out of the ownership, maintenance, or use of a covered auto. We will settle or defend any claim or suit for property damage as we consider appropriate.

Limit of Liability: $50,000 per accident

1.3 Supplementary Payments
In addition to our limit of liability, we will pay on behalf of an insured:
a) Up to $250 for the cost of bail bonds required because of an accident.
b) Premiums on appeal bonds and bonds to release attachments in any suit we defend.
c) Interest accruing after a judgment is entered in any suit we defend.
d) Up to $200 per day for loss of earnings, but not other income, because of attendance at hearings or trials at our request.
e) Other reasonable expenses incurred at our request.

SECTION 2: MEDICAL PAYMENTS COVERAGE

2.1 Coverage Description
We will pay reasonable expenses incurred for necessary medical and funeral services because of bodily injury caused by accident and sustained by an insured. Medical expenses must be incurred within three years from the date of the accident.

2.2 Coverage Limits
Medical Payments Limit: $5,000 per person per accident

2.3 Exclusions
Medical Payments Coverage does not apply to bodily injury:
a) Sustained while occupying any motorized vehicle having fewer than four wheels.
b) Sustained while occupying a vehicle owned by the named insured that is not a covered auto.
c) Sustained while occupying a vehicle used as a public or livery conveyance.
d) Occurring during the course of employment if workers compensation benefits are available.
e) Caused by a nuclear weapon, war, civil unrest, or radioactive contamination.

SECTION 3: UNINSURED/UNDERINSURED MOTORIST COVERAGE

3.1 Uninsured Motorist Coverage
We will pay compensatory damages which an insured is legally entitled to recover from the owner or operator of an uninsured motor vehicle because of bodily injury sustained by an insured and caused by an accident.

3.2 Underinsured Motorist Coverage
We will pay compensatory damages which an insured is legally entitled to recover from the owner or operator of an underinsured motor vehicle because of bodily injury sustained by an insured and caused by an accident. An underinsured motor vehicle is one with liability limits less than the limits of this coverage.

3.3 Determining Fault
If any insured and we do not agree on the insured's entitlement to damages or the amount of damages, either party may demand arbitration. Each party will select an arbitrator. The two arbitrators will select a third. If they cannot agree within 30 days, either may request that a judge select the third arbitrator. Each party will pay the expenses of its own arbitrator and bear the expenses of the third arbitrator equally.

SECTION 4: COLLISION AND COMPREHENSIVE COVERAGE

4.1 Collision Coverage
We will pay for direct and accidental loss to a covered auto or any non-owned auto, including its equipment, minus any applicable deductible, caused by collision. Collision means the upset of the covered auto or its impact with another vehicle or object.

Deductible: $500

4.2 Comprehensive Coverage
We will pay for direct and accidental loss to a covered auto or any non-owned auto, including its equipment, minus any applicable deductible, caused by any peril other than collision. This includes theft, fire, hail, windstorm, vandalism, flood, falling objects, missiles, explosion, earthquake, contact with a bird or animal, and glass breakage.

Deductible: $250

4.3 Coverage Exclusions
We will not pay for:
a) Loss to a covered auto while it is used to carry persons or property for a fee (rideshare services excluded if rideshare endorsement is attached).
b) Damage due to wear and tear, freezing, mechanical or electrical breakdown or failure.
c) Tires unless damaged by fire, vandalism, or theft, or unless the tire damage occurs simultaneously with other covered damage.
d) Loss due to radar detectors, custom equipment, or aftermarket modifications unless specifically scheduled.
e) Loss to any electronic equipment designed for the reproduction of sound unless permanently installed in the auto.
f) Loss to tapes, records, discs, or similar media devices.

SECTION 5: DUTIES AFTER AN ACCIDENT OR LOSS

5.1 General Duties
In the event of an accident or loss, the insured must:
a) Promptly notify us or our agent of how, when, and where the accident or loss happened.
b) Cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement, or defense of any claim or suit.
c) Promptly send us copies of any notices or legal papers received in connection with the accident or loss.
d) Submit to physical examinations by physicians selected by us at our expense as often as we reasonably require.

5.2 Duties for Collision and Comprehensive Claims
In addition to the general duties, the insured must:
a) Take reasonable steps to protect the covered auto from further damage and allow us to inspect the auto before repair.
b) File a police report if the auto is stolen.
c) Provide us with documentation, including repair estimates and photographs, as reasonably requested.

SECTION 6: GENERAL PROVISIONS

6.1 Policy Territory
This policy applies only to accidents and losses which occur within the United States of America, its territories and possessions, Puerto Rico, and Canada.

6.2 Cancellation
The named insured may cancel this policy at any time by returning it to us or by written notification of the date cancellation is to take effect. We may cancel this policy by mailing written notice to the named insured at least 30 days before the cancellation date.

6.3 Non-Renewal
If we decide not to renew this policy, we will mail written notice to the named insured at least 30 days before the expiration date.

6.4 Premium Calculation
The premium for this policy is based on information we received from you and other sources. Factors affecting premium include driving record, vehicle type and year, garaging location, annual mileage, credit history where permitted by law, and coverage selections.

Annual Premium: $1,847.00
