Document 1: AUTO INSURANCE POLICY
Document Name: auto_policy.txt
================================================================================

SECTION 1: COVERAGE TYPES AND LIMITS

1.1 Bodily Injury Liability Coverage
Bodily injury liability coverage applies to bodily injury or death caused by an accident 
that you are legally liable for. This coverage pays for medical expenses, rehabilitation 
costs, lost wages, and pain and suffering of other parties involved in an accident caused 
by your vehicle.

Coverage Limits:
- Per Person Limit: $25,000
- Per Accident Limit: $50,000
- This means we pay up to $25,000 per injured person and up to $50,000 total per accident

1.2 Property Damage Liability Coverage
Property damage liability covers damage to someone else's property caused by your vehicle. 
This includes damage to other vehicles, buildings, fences, utility poles, and personal property.

Coverage Limits:
- Per Accident Limit: $25,000
The policyholder may select higher limits based on their needs and asset protection goals.

1.3 Collision Coverage
Collision coverage applies to damage from collision with another vehicle or object. This covers 
repair or replacement costs for your vehicle regardless of who is at fault in the accident.

Deductible Options:
- The policyholder may select a deductible of $250, $500, or $1,000
- If no deductible is specified, the default is $500
- The deductible applies per occurrence (per accident)
- Higher deductibles result in lower monthly premiums

Maximum Coverage Limit: $50,000 per occurrence, $100,000 per policy year

1.4 Comprehensive Coverage
Comprehensive coverage protects against non-collision damages including theft, vandalism, 
natural disasters, and weather-related incidents. This coverage applies to losses caused by 
fire, explosion, falling objects, missiles, or any other cause not involving collision.

Deductible Options: Same as collision coverage ($250, $500, $1,000)
Maximum Coverage Limit: $50,000 per occurrence

SECTION 2: ADDITIONAL COVERAGES

2.1 Uninsured Motorist Coverage
Uninsured motorist coverage protects you and your passengers if you are hit by an uninsured 
or underinsured driver. This coverage pays for medical expenses and lost wages up to your 
selected limit.

Coverage Limit: $15,000 per person, $30,000 per accident

2.2 Medical Payments Coverage
Medical payments coverage pays for medical and funeral expenses for you and your passengers 
following an auto accident, regardless of fault.

Coverage Limit: $5,000 per person
Covered Expenses:
- Ambulance services
- Hospital room charges
- Surgical fees
- X-rays and laboratory tests
- Dental services
- Funeral expenses

2.3 Rideshare Coverage
Rideshare coverage applies when you are driving for rideshare services like Uber or Lyft. 
This coverage extends liability and collision protection while you are actively engaged in 
rideshare services.

Important Note: Standard auto insurance does not cover rideshare activities. You must add 
this endorsement to your policy for protection during rideshare operations.

SECTION 3: EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS

3.1 General Exclusions
The following are NOT covered under this policy:

- Intentional damage caused by you or household members
- Wear and tear, regular maintenance, or mechanical failures
- Damage from racing or speed contests
- Using your vehicle for commercial purposes (except for rideshare if endorsed)
- Damage caused while driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs
- Violations of traffic laws at the time of loss
- Damage caused by nuclear reaction or radiation
- Damage to custom equipment or aftermarket parts not specifically endorsed

3.2 Collision Exclusions
Collision coverage does NOT pay for:

- Damage from hitting an animal (covered under comprehensive)
- Damage from weather conditions alone (covered under comprehensive)
- Damage from hitting a stationary object in a parking lot after leaving the scene (hit and run)
- Mechanical or electrical breakdown

SECTION 4: CLAIMS PROCEDURE

4.1 Reporting Requirements
You must report any accident or loss to us within 30 days of the incident. Failure to report 
promptly may result in denial of your claim.

Required Information:
- Date, time, and location of accident
- Description of what happened
- Names and contact information of other parties involved
- Contact information of any witnesses
- Police report number (if applicable)
- Photographs of damage (if safe to obtain)

4.2 Claims Investigation
We reserve the right to investigate any claim thoroughly. This may include:

- Inspection of your vehicle by our adjusters
- Review of medical records (for injury claims)
- Obtaining statements from witnesses
- Determining fault based on evidence collected

4.3 Payment of Claims
Once approved, we will pay the claim within 15 business days. Payment will be made to:

- You (the policyholder), OR
- You and the lienholder jointly (if there is an outstanding loan on your vehicle), OR
- Directly to the repair shop (if authorized by you)

Document 2: HEALTH INSURANCE POLICY
Document Name: health_policy.txt
================================================================================

SECTION 1: COVERAGE OVERVIEW

1.1 Plan Summary
This comprehensive health insurance policy provides coverage for preventive care, medical 
services, prescription drugs, mental health services, and emergency care. Coverage includes 
services provided by in-network and out-of-network providers.

1.2 Individual Annual Deductible
The individual annual deductible is $1,500. This means you must pay the first $1,500 of your 
covered medical expenses each calendar year before insurance benefits begin.

Special Note: 
- Preventive care services (annual checkups, vaccinations, screenings) are covered at 100% 
  regardless of deductible
- The deductible resets on January 1st of each year
- Once you meet your deductible, you pay only your coinsurance (percentage) for covered services

1.3 Family Annual Deductible
If you have family coverage, the family deductible is $3,000. Once any family member accumulates 
$3,000 in individual deductibles, the entire family deductible is considered met.

1.4 Out-of-Pocket Maximum
The annual out-of-pocket maximum (including deductible and coinsurance) is $5,000 per individual 
and $10,000 per family. Once you reach this limit, we pay 100% of covered services for the 
remainder of the calendar year.

SECTION 2: COVERED SERVICES

2.1 Preventive Care Services (Covered at 100%)
The following preventive care services are covered with no cost-sharing:

- Annual physical examination
- Preventive health screenings (cholesterol, blood pressure, diabetes screening)
- Cancer screenings (mammography, colonoscopy, cervical cancer screening)
- Immunizations and vaccinations
- Prenatal and postnatal care
- Well-child visits up to age 21
- Mental health screening
- Substance abuse screening

2.2 Office Visits and Primary Care
- In-network office visits: $30 copay per visit
- Out-of-network office visits: $60 copay per visit after deductible
- Urgent care visits: $50 copay
- Emergency room visits: $300 copay (waived if admitted to hospital)

2.3 Prescription Drug Coverage
Prescription drugs are covered under a tiered formulary system:

Tier 1 (Generic Drugs): $10 copay
Tier 2 (Preferred Brand-Name): $30 copay
Tier 3 (Non-Preferred Brand-Name): $50 copay
Tier 4 (Specialty Drugs): 25% coinsurance with $150 minimum copay

Note: Some medications require prior authorization before coverage is provided.

2.4 Mental Health and Substance Abuse Services
Mental health and substance abuse treatment is covered at parity with physical health services.

Covered Services:
- Inpatient mental health treatment: 60% coinsurance after deductible
- Outpatient mental health visits: $30 copay per visit
- Psychiatric medications: Covered under prescription drug benefits
- Substance abuse counseling: $30 copay per visit
- Inpatient substance abuse treatment: 60% coinsurance after deductible
- Telehealth mental health visits: $20 copay per visit

Maximum Benefits: Up to 30 outpatient visits per calendar year

2.5 Hospitalization and Inpatient Services
- Hospital admission copay: $300 per admission
- Inpatient hospital care: 80% coinsurance after deductible
- Surgical procedures: 80% coinsurance after deductible
- Anesthesia: Covered as part of surgical procedure
- Diagnostic imaging (X-ray, CT, MRI): $50 copay (in-network), 80% coinsurance (out-of-network)

2.6 Maternity and Newborn Care
Maternity and newborn care is fully covered with no cost-sharing:

- Prenatal visits: Covered at 100%
- Labor and delivery: 80% coinsurance after deductible
- Postpartum care: Covered at 100%
- Newborn screening and vaccinations: Covered at 100%
- Breastfeeding support and counseling: Covered at 100%

SECTION 3: EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS

3.1 Services NOT Covered
The following services are NOT covered under this policy:

- Cosmetic procedures (unless medically necessary)
- Weight loss programs and bariatric surgery (unless specifically authorized)
- Fertility treatments and assisted reproductive procedures
- Hair transplants
- Routine dental and vision care (covered under separate riders if purchased)
- Experimental or investigational treatments not approved by FDA
- Services provided by non-licensed providers
- Over-the-counter medications not prescribed by a physician

3.2 Pre-Existing Condition Limitations
Pre-existing conditions are covered from the first day of coverage. No waiting period applies 
to treatment of pre-existing conditions.

Definition: A pre-existing condition is any health condition diagnosed or treated within 
12 months before your coverage starts.

3.3 Mental Health Service Limitations
- Annual visit limit: 30 outpatient visits per calendar year
- Inpatient mental health treatment: Limited to 60 days per calendar year
- Substance abuse treatment: Limited to 30 days inpatient or 40 outpatient visits per year

SECTION 4: CLAIM SUBMISSION AND PAYMENT

4.1 Filing Requirements
Claims must be submitted within 180 days of the date of service. Claims submitted after 
this deadline may be denied.

Required Documentation:
- Itemized bill from provider
- Explanation of any procedures performed
- Proof of payment
- Medical records (if requested)

4.2 Claim Decision Timeline
- Clean claims: We will make a decision within 30 days of receipt
- Claims requiring additional information: 45 days
- Claims under dispute: 60 days

4.3 Appeal Process
If a claim is denied, you have the right to appeal within 180 days. The appeal will be reviewed 
by a different physician than the original reviewer.

Document 3: HOMEOWNERS INSURANCE POLICY
Document Name: home_policy.txt
================================================================================

SECTION 1: DWELLING AND STRUCTURE COVERAGE

1.1 Dwelling Coverage Limit
Dwelling coverage protects your home's structure against covered perils. The dwelling coverage 
limit for this policy is $300,000.

This includes protection for:
- The main house structure
- Attached structures (attached garage, deck, etc.)
- Built-in fixtures and permanent installations
- Roof and roof materials
- Foundation and foundation materials

1.2 Coverage for Additional Structures
Additional structures on your property (detached garage, shed, pool house) are covered at 
10% of the dwelling limit, or $30,000 in this case.

This does NOT include:
- Fences and gates
- Underground structures
- Structures used for business purposes

SECTION 2: PERSONAL PROPERTY COVERAGE

2.1 Personal Property Coverage Limit
Personal property coverage protects your belongings inside and outside the home up to 70% 
of dwelling coverage, or $210,000 in this case.

Covered Items:
- Furniture and furnishings
- Clothing and personal items
- Kitchen appliances
- Electronics and computer equipment
- Books, artwork, and collectibles
- Lawn and garden equipment

2.2 High-Value Item Coverage
Certain items have sub-limits and may require scheduling:

- Jewelry: Limited to $2,500 total
- Furs: Limited to $2,500 total
- Firearms: Limited to $2,500 total
- Silverware and precious metals: Limited to $5,000 total
- Fine arts and paintings: Limited to $5,000 total
- Business equipment: Limited to $2,500 total

To increase these limits, items must be scheduled separately on the policy with appraisals.

SECTION 3: DEDUCTIBLES

3.1 All-Peril Deductible
The standard deductible for all covered perils is $1,000. This applies to each separate loss.

3.2 Hurricane/Wind Deductible
For wind and hail damage (including hurricane-related wind), a separate deductible of 5% 
of dwelling coverage applies, which equals $15,000 on this policy.

The 5% hurricane deductible is significantly higher than the standard deductible and applies 
specifically to losses from:
- Hurricane force winds (74+ mph)
- Tornado damage
- Hail damage
- Wind damage

3.3 Earthquake Deductible
Earthquake damage is NOT included in standard homeowners coverage. A separate earthquake 
insurance rider with a 15% deductible is available for an additional premium.

SECTION 4: COVERED PERILS

4.1 Standard Covered Perils
This policy covers direct physical loss from the following perils:

- Fire and smoke damage
- Lightning and electrical damage
- Explosion
- Windstorm (excluding hurricane-force winds separately deductible)
- Hail
- Theft and burglary
- Vandalism and malicious mischief
- Falling objects
- Weight of snow, ice, or sleet
- Water damage (excluding flood and ground water)
- Collapse of building
- Glass breakage (windows and glass doors)

4.2 EXCLUDED Perils - What is NOT Covered
The following perils are specifically EXCLUDED from coverage:

- Flood damage (requires separate flood insurance policy)
- Groundwater or seepage
- Earthquake and earth movement
- War and civil unrest
- Neglect and lack of maintenance
- Settling, cracking, or shrinkage of the structure
- Mold and fungus (with limited exception)
- Damage from rodents, insects, or vermin
- Damage from inherent defects
- Damage from continuous or repeated water seepage
- Nuclear hazard
- Intentional loss caused by homeowner

4.3 Flood Damage Exclusion - IMPORTANT
Flood damage is NOT covered under standard homeowners insurance. Flood includes:

- Water overflowing from rivers, lakes, or coastal areas
- Water driven by storm surge
- Accumulated water from heavy rain on ground level
- Water backing up from sewers or drains

To obtain flood coverage, you must purchase a separate National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) 
policy or private flood insurance policy.

SECTION 5: LIABILITY COVERAGE

5.1 Personal Liability Coverage
Personal liability coverage protects you if someone is injured on your property or if you 
accidentally damage someone else's property.

Coverage Limit: $300,000 per occurrence
This covers:
- Medical expenses of injured visitors
- Legal defense costs
- Damages you are legally liable for
- Pain and suffering of injured parties

5.2 Medical Payments to Others
Medical payments coverage pays for accidental injuries to others on your property, regardless 
of fault. This is separate from liability and does not require proof of legal liability.

Coverage Limit: $5,000 per person, per incident
Covered Expenses:
- Ambulance and emergency room care
- Hospital stay
- Surgical procedures
- X-rays and diagnostic tests
- Dental services
- Funeral expenses (if injury is fatal)

SECTION 6: CLAIMS PROCEDURE

6.1 Duties After Loss
You must take the following steps after any loss:

1. Immediately notify us of any loss (ideally within 24 hours)
2. Protect the property from further damage (mitigation)
3. Cooperate fully with our investigation
4. Provide accurate information about the loss
5. Provide documentation and receipts
6. Allow us to inspect the property and damages
7. Provide police reports for theft or vandalism (within 30 days)

Failure to comply with these duties may result in denial of your claim.

6.2 Claim Settlement Options
For covered losses, we will settle your claim using one of the following methods:

Option 1: Replacement Cost
We pay for the cost to replace damaged items with new items of similar quality. This is 
the standard settlement method.

Option 2: Actual Cash Value
We pay for the current market value of damaged items, accounting for depreciation. This 
results in lower payouts than replacement cost.

Option 3: Repair Estimate
For structural damage, we may pay based on the lowest competitive repair estimate obtained.

6.3 Claim Timeline
- Claim acknowledgment: Within 5 business days of notice
- Claim decision: Within 30 days for simple claims, 60+ days for complex claims
- Payment: Within 10 business days of claim approval

SECTION 7: SPECIAL PROVISIONS

7.1 Home Security Discount
Homes with approved security systems (burglar alarm, fire alarm) qualify for a 10% discount 
on the policy premium.

7.2 Replacement Cost Endorsement
This policy includes automatic replacement cost coverage for contents (not cash value). Your 
personal property is covered for the full replacement cost, not depreciated value.

7.3 Updated Replacement Cost
The dwelling coverage limit automatically increases by 2% each year to account for inflation 
and construction cost increases.
